# Privacy Policy

## Biblomnēmon - Reading Tracker

Last updated: 30.12.2025

## Overview

Biblomnēmon is a reading tracker application designed to help users build and manage a personal book library and track their reading activity. The app is built with a strong focus on privacy, data ownership, and minimal data collection.

This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and how it is stored.

## Data Collection

Biblomnēmon does not require account registration and does not collect personal identification data such as name, email address, phone number, or location.

The app stores the following data locally on your device:

- Book information you add (such as title, author, ISBN)
- Reading progress and reading history
- App-related preferences

All data is created and managed by the user.

## Camera Usage (ISBN Scanning)

Biblomnēmon uses the device camera only for scanning ISBN barcodes to help users quickly add or search for books.

- Camera access is used exclusively for barcode scanning
- No images or videos are stored
- No camera data is transmitted or shared

## Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device.

Biblomnēmon does not upload, transmit, or synchronize data to any external servers or cloud services.

## Data Sharing

Biblomnēmon:

- Does not sell user data
- Does not share user data with third parties
- Does not use advertising SDKs
- Does not use analytics or tracking services
- And never will be

## Children's Privacy

Biblomnēmon does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, the app can be used without providing personal information.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's functionality changes. Any updates will be published at this same location.
